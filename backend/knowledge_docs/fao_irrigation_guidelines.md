# FAO Irrigation and Drainage Guidelines (Paper 56)
Source: Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the United Nations
Category: Irrigation Scheduling & Soil Water Depletion

## Section 1: Soil Moisture Depletion Limits
- Critical Soil Moisture Depletion (p-factor): For most cereal and vegetable crops, readily available water (RAW) is depleted when volumetric soil moisture drops below 20% to 25%.
- Severe Drought Stress (< 15% Volumetric Moisture): When soil moisture declines below 15%, plants enter permanent wilting point risk. Stomatal closure occurs, causing immediate reduction in photosynthesis and crop yield loss.
- Recommended Remediative Action: Initiate prompt controlled drip or sprinkler irrigation for 25 to 40 minutes to restore root-zone field capacity to the optimal range (35% - 55%).

## Section 2: Evaporative Demand & Weather Adjustment
- If solar radiation is high and ambient temperature exceeds 30°C, increase irrigation duration by 15-20% to offset atmospheric vapor pressure deficit (VPD).
- Avoid excessive continuous irrigation (> 60 minutes) in a single cycle to prevent deep percolation water loss, nutrient leaching, and root hypoxia.
