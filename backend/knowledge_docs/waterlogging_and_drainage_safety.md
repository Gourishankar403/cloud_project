# Agricultural Drainage and Anaerobic Soil Prevention Standards
Source: International Commission on Irrigation and Drainage (ICID)
Category: Soil Saturation & Drainage Safety

## Section 1: Soil Saturation and Oversaturation (> 70%)
- When volumetric soil moisture exceeds 70%, air voids in soil pores are displaced by water, depriving plant roots of dissolved oxygen.
- Anaerobic fermentation begins within 24 hours of root submergence, triggering Pythium root rot and nutrient uptake arrest.
- Safety Mandate: Cease all irrigation pump operations immediately. Inspect drainage channels and culverts.
- Never authorize automated irrigation commands if soil moisture exceeds 65%.
