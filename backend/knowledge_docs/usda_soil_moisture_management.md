# USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS)
Source: United States Department of Agriculture - National Water & Climate Center
Category: Soil Water Tension & Irrigation Water Management

## Section 1: Soil Texture & Moisture Thresholds
- Loamy and Silty Soils: Ideal moisture retention is 40% to 65%. Available Water Capacity (AWC) critical management threshold is 20%.
- Below Threshold Response (< 20%): Soil water matric potential drops below -100 kPa. Root uptake resistance increases exponentially.
- Emergency Action: Controlled automated irrigation pulse. Minimum recommended irrigation cycle is 20 minutes; maximum safe continuous application is 45 minutes to prevent surface runoff and erosion.

## Section 2: Sensor Calibration & Diagnostic Interpretation
- Sensor readings below 18% in active root zones require validation against atmospheric evapotranspiration indices.
- If no imminent precipitation is forecasted within 12 hours, trigger immediate soil hydration to protect vegetative stages.
