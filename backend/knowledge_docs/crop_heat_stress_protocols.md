# Precision Agriculture Heat Stress Mitigation Guidelines
Source: International Center for Agricultural Research in Dry Areas (ICARDA)
Category: High Temperature & Evapotranspiration Defense

## Section 1: Elevated Ambient Temperature (> 35°C)
- When air temperature surpasses 35°C (95°F), crop canopy thermal stress spikes. Transpiration water loss accelerates drastically.
- Interaction with Soil Moisture: If soil moisture is concurrently below 25%, thermal stress induces rapid vascular cavitation.
- Recommended Interventions:
  1. Micro-sprinkler canopy cooling or supplemental localized root hydration for 15-30 minutes.
  2. Mulching and canopy shading inspection.
  3. Continuous 5-minute telemetry polling to observe rate of soil moisture decline.

## Section 2: Nocturnal Temperature Inversion
- Elevated nighttime temperatures prevent plant respiratory recovery. Pre-dawn root-zone moisture should be maintained at minimum 40%.
